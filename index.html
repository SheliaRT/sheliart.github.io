About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Wayne State University, working in the Machine Learning and Health Informatics Laboratory under the supervision of Dr. Suzan Arslanturk. My research focuses on machine learning, domain adaptation, and data integration for heterogeneous, high-dimensional datasets — particularly transcriptomic and healthcare data collected across different sources, platforms, and cohorts.

My work spans kernel methods, graph neural networks, active learning, transformer-based models, and interpretable machine learning, with the goal of building models that generalize across domains while preserving biologically meaningful signal. I am currently developing transformer-based methods for integrating single-cell RNA sequencing and spatial transcriptomics data.

Before my Ph.D., I was a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at Gono Bishwabidyalay, Bangladesh. I am expected to graduate in May 2027 and am actively seeking AI research positions.
